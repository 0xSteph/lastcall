How it works

Three steps. The last one only happens if you need it.

1

Tell it the plan

What you're doing, when you'll be back, and who to tell: one to five people, in order. Add your route, where you parked, your starting location.

2

Check in when you're back

One tap on I'm safe, from your plan page or right on the reminder push. Running late? Tap +30 min. Nobody is bothered.

3

Miss it, and they find out

After a short grace period your first contact gets an urgent alert with your plan and location. No answer? The next one does. “I'm on it” stops the chain, and your “I'm safe” sends everyone an all clear.

Why Step Functions

One workflow per plan. Waiting is free.

Each plan is a single Standard Step Functions execution running one small loop. It sleeps until the next milestone (reminder, deadline, grace period, next contact) and wakes early only when someone acts.

The timeout is the schedule

WaitForEvent waits for a task token with TimeoutSecondsPath set to the next milestone. No cron, no polling, no queues.

The API never sends notifications

Endpoints change the plan in DynamoDB with a conditional write, then nudge the execution with SendTaskSuccess. Every side effect happens in Advance.

Once, even on retries

Each alert is marked sent with a conditional write before it goes out, so retries and races never double-notify anyone.

Idle costs nothing

HTTP API, Lambda, DynamoDB, Step Functions, S3 and CloudFront. A plan that's waiting six hours is just a paused execution.
