Tell Last Call what you're doing, when you'll be back and who to tell. Check in by then and nothing happens.

1What are you doing?

Shown to contacts as a call button if they're alerted.
Only shown on their alert page. Simple formatting works: **bold**, lists with -, links.

2When will you be back?

You get an urgent ping first. Contacts are alerted after this.

3Who should we tell?

Contacts are alerted one at a time, in this order, until someone says “I'm on it”. Up to 5.
